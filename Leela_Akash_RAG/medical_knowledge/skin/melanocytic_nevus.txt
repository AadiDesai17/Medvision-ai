Condition: Melanocytic Nevus

Dataset: HAM10000
Category: Skin lesion

General Information:
A melanocytic nevus, commonly called a mole, is a usually benign growth of melanocytes. Nevi can vary in size, shape, and color and may occur anywhere on the skin.

Common Features:
A typical nevus may be a small, well-defined, evenly colored lesion with a relatively regular shape. Some nevi are present from birth, while others develop during childhood or adulthood.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
Genetic factors and ultraviolet exposure can influence the development and number of nevi. People with many moles or atypical moles may have a higher risk of melanoma.

General Management:
Most typical nevi do not require treatment. A changing, unusual, or symptomatic lesion should be evaluated by a healthcare professional and may require dermoscopy or biopsy.
