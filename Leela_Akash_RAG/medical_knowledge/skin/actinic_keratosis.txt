Condition: Actinic Keratosis

Dataset: HAM10000
Category: Skin lesion

General Information:
Actinic keratosis is a rough, scaly skin lesion caused by long-term exposure to ultraviolet radiation. It commonly develops on sun-exposed areas of the skin.

Common Features:
Lesions may appear as rough or scaly patches and can vary in color. They are commonly found on the face, ears, scalp, forearms, and hands.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
Long-term sun exposure is the main risk factor. Risk is also influenced by age, fair skin, and a history of significant ultraviolet exposure.

General Management:
Management may include monitoring, cryotherapy, topical treatments, or other procedures depending on the number and characteristics of lesions. Clinical evaluation is recommended because some lesions can progress to skin cancer.
