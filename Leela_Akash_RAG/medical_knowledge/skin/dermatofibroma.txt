Condition: Dermatofibroma

Dataset: HAM10000
Category: Skin lesion

General Information:
Dermatofibroma is a common benign skin growth that usually develops in the deeper layers of the skin. It is generally harmless and often remains stable over time.

Common Features:
It commonly appears as a small, firm, raised lesion that may be brown, pink, or another skin color. Some lesions may show a characteristic dimpling when the surrounding skin is pinched.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
The exact cause is not always known. Some dermatofibromas may develop after minor skin injury or insect bites.

General Management:
Most dermatofibromas do not require treatment. Removal may be considered if the lesion is painful, repeatedly irritated, or if its diagnosis is uncertain.
