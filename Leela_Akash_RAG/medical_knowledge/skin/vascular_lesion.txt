Condition: Vascular Lesion

Dataset: HAM10000
Category: Skin lesion

General Information:
Vascular lesions are skin abnormalities involving blood vessels. They can be benign and may appear as red, purple, or bluish areas or growths on the skin.

Common Features:
Appearance varies depending on the type of vascular lesion. Lesions may be flat or raised and can range from small spots to larger areas of discoloration.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
Causes vary by lesion type. Some vascular lesions are present from birth, while others develop later in life due to changes in blood vessels or other factors.

General Management:
Many benign vascular lesions require no treatment. Depending on the type, location, symptoms, and cosmetic concerns, treatment may include observation, laser therapy, or other procedures. Clinical evaluation may be needed when the diagnosis is uncertain.
