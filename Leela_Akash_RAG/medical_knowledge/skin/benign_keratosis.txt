Condition: Benign Keratosis-like Lesion

Dataset: HAM10000
Category: Skin lesion

General Information:
Benign keratosis-like lesions are non-cancerous skin growths that can have different appearances and may resemble other pigmented or scaly skin lesions.

Common Features:
They may appear as well-defined, raised, scaly, waxy, or pigmented lesions. Their color can range from light to dark and their appearance may vary between individuals.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
Risk factors vary by lesion type. Some benign keratotic lesions become more common with increasing age and may occur on different areas of the body.

General Management:
Many benign lesions do not require treatment. If a lesion changes in appearance, becomes symptomatic, or is difficult to distinguish from a malignant lesion, clinical assessment may be recommended.
