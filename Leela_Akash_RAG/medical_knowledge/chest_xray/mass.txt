Condition: Lung Mass

Dataset: ChestX-ray14
Category: Chest X-ray finding

General Information:
A lung mass is a relatively large abnormal area or lesion seen within the lung on medical imaging. A mass is an imaging finding and does not by itself establish a specific diagnosis.

Common Features:
A chest X-ray may show a localized abnormal opacity or rounded lesion. Further imaging is often needed to characterize its size, shape, location, and other features.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
Possible causes include benign growths, infections, inflammatory conditions, and malignant tumors. Risk factors depend on the underlying cause.

General Management:
An identified lung mass generally requires clinical evaluation and appropriate follow-up imaging or testing. Management depends on the final diagnosis and may include observation, additional imaging, biopsy, or treatment of an identified condition.
