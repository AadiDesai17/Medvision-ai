Condition: Pulmonary Nodule

Dataset: ChestX-ray14
Category: Chest X-ray finding

General Information:
A pulmonary nodule is a small, rounded or irregular area of increased density within the lung. Many pulmonary nodules are benign, but some may require further evaluation.

Common Features:
A nodule may appear as a small localized opacity on chest imaging. Its size, shape, location, and changes over time can help clinicians determine the appropriate evaluation.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
Possible causes include previous infections, inflammatory conditions, benign growths, and lung cancer. Risk depends on factors such as age, smoking history, and other clinical features.

General Management:
Management depends on the nodule's characteristics and the patient's clinical risk. Follow-up imaging or additional diagnostic evaluation may be recommended.
