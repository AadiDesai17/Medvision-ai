Condition: Pleural Effusion

Dataset: ChestX-ray14
Category: Chest X-ray finding

General Information:
Pleural effusion is an abnormal accumulation of fluid in the pleural space around the lungs. It can occur due to several underlying conditions and may affect breathing.

Common Features:
On a chest X-ray, pleural effusion may appear as increased opacity at the lower part of the chest with blunting of the costophrenic angle. Larger effusions may cause shortness of breath, cough, or chest discomfort.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
Common causes include heart failure, infection, inflammation, kidney or liver disease, and certain cancers.

General Management:
Management depends on the cause and severity. Treatment may involve treating the underlying condition and, when necessary, removing pleural fluid for diagnosis or symptom relief.
