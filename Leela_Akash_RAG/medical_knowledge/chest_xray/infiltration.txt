Condition: Infiltration

Dataset: ChestX-ray14
Category: Chest X-ray finding

General Information:
Infiltration is a radiographic description used for an area of increased or abnormal lung opacity. It may reflect different underlying processes, including infection, inflammation, fluid, or other lung abnormalities.

Common Features:
Chest X-ray may show patchy or diffuse areas of increased lung opacity. The appearance and distribution can vary depending on the underlying cause.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
Possible causes include respiratory infections, inflammatory conditions, pulmonary edema, and other processes affecting the lung tissue.

General Management:
Management depends on the underlying cause. Clinical assessment and additional tests or imaging may be needed to determine the cause and appropriate treatment.
