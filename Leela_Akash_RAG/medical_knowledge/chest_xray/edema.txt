Condition: Pulmonary Edema

Dataset: ChestX-ray14
Category: Chest X-ray finding

General Information:
Pulmonary edema occurs when excess fluid accumulates in the lung tissue and air spaces. It can interfere with normal oxygen exchange and breathing.

Common Features:
Chest X-ray may show bilateral or diffuse increased lung opacity, vascular congestion, and other features depending on the severity and cause. Symptoms may include shortness of breath and difficulty breathing.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
A common cause is heart failure. Other causes can include kidney problems, severe illness, certain medications, or conditions that injure the lungs.

General Management:
Management depends on the underlying cause and severity. Treatment may include oxygen or other supportive care and treatment of the condition causing the edema.
