Condition: Pneumothorax

Dataset: ChestX-ray14
Category: Chest X-ray finding

General Information:
Pneumothorax occurs when air enters the pleural space between the lung and chest wall. This can cause partial or complete collapse of the affected lung.

Common Features:
A chest X-ray may show a visible pleural line with absent lung markings beyond it. Symptoms may include sudden chest pain and shortness of breath.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
Pneumothorax may occur spontaneously or result from chest injury, medical procedures, or underlying lung disease. Risk factors depend on the type and cause.

General Management:
Management depends on the size and severity of the pneumothorax. Small cases may be observed, while larger or symptomatic cases may require removal of air from the pleural space.
