Condition: Pneumonia

Dataset: ChestX-ray14
Category: Chest X-ray finding

General Information:
Pneumonia is an infection that causes inflammation in the air sacs of one or both lungs. The air sacs may fill with fluid or inflammatory material, which can affect breathing.

Common Features:
Chest X-ray may show areas of increased opacity or consolidation in the lungs. Symptoms can include cough, fever, chills, chest pain, and difficulty breathing.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
Pneumonia can be caused by bacteria, viruses, fungi, or other infectious organisms. Risk varies with age, underlying health conditions, immune status, and exposure to infectious agents.

General Management:
Management depends on the cause and severity. Bacterial pneumonia may require antibiotics, while supportive care may be used for other causes. Severe cases may require hospital treatment.
