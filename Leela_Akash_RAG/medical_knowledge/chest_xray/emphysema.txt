Condition: Emphysema

Dataset: ChestX-ray14
Category: Chest X-ray finding

General Information:
Emphysema is a chronic lung condition in which the walls of the air sacs are damaged, reducing the surface area available for normal gas exchange. It is a major form of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.

Common Features:
Chest imaging may show increased lung volumes and other changes associated with chronic air trapping. Symptoms commonly include shortness of breath, cough, and reduced exercise tolerance.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
Long-term cigarette smoking is a major risk factor. Other exposures and, less commonly, inherited conditions can also contribute.

General Management:
Management may include smoking cessation, appropriate inhaled medicines, pulmonary rehabilitation, vaccinations, and treatment of exacerbations. Severe disease may require additional interventions.
