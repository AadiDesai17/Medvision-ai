Condition: Hernia

Dataset: ChestX-ray14
Category: Chest X-ray finding

General Information:
A hernia occurs when an organ or tissue pushes through a weak area in the surrounding muscle or connective tissue. In chest imaging, a diaphragmatic hernia may allow abdominal contents to move into the chest.

Common Features:
Imaging may show an abnormal position of abdominal structures within the chest or an unusual contour of the diaphragm. Symptoms depend on the type and size of the hernia.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
Hernias may be congenital or develop later in life. Risk factors vary and may include increased abdominal pressure, previous surgery, injury, or weakness of supporting tissues.

General Management:
Management depends on the type, size, symptoms, and risk of complications. Clinical evaluation and additional imaging may be required. Some hernias may require surgical repair.
