Condition: Cardiomegaly

Dataset: ChestX-ray14
Category: Chest X-ray finding

General Information:
Cardiomegaly means an enlarged heart. It is a finding that may be seen on chest imaging and can be associated with several underlying heart or systemic conditions.

Common Features:
On a chest X-ray, an enlarged cardiac silhouette may suggest cardiomegaly. Clinical symptoms depend on the underlying cause and may include shortness of breath, fatigue, swelling of the legs, or reduced exercise tolerance.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
Possible causes include high blood pressure, heart valve disease, cardiomyopathy, coronary artery disease, and other conditions that place increased workload on the heart.

General Management:
Management depends on the underlying cause. Evaluation may include clinical assessment, electrocardiography, echocardiography, blood tests, or additional imaging. Treatment is directed at the underlying condition.
