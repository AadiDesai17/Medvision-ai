Condition: Mild Diabetic Retinopathy

Dataset: EyePACS
Category: Diabetic retinopathy grade

General Information:
Mild diabetic retinopathy corresponds to an early stage of diabetic retinal disease. It is commonly associated with the presence of small retinal blood vessel abnormalities called microaneurysms.

Common Features:
Small microaneurysms may be visible in retinal photographs. At this stage, significant vision loss may not be present, although progression is possible.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
Diabetic retinopathy is associated with diabetes. Longer duration of diabetes and factors such as poor blood glucose control, high blood pressure, and abnormal blood lipid levels can increase risk.

General Management:
Management generally focuses on controlling diabetes and other risk factors and maintaining recommended retinal screening and follow-up.
