Condition: No Diabetic Retinopathy

Dataset: EyePACS
Category: Diabetic retinopathy grade

General Information:
No diabetic retinopathy means that the retinal image does not show characteristic signs of diabetic retinopathy according to the classification category.

Common Features:
The retina may appear without the characteristic abnormalities used to classify diabetic retinopathy. Clinical assessment may still consider other eye conditions and the patient's overall health.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
No diabetic retinopathy is a classification result rather than a disease. People with diabetes can develop retinopathy over time, so regular eye examinations are important.

General Management:
For people with diabetes, recommended management includes appropriate diabetes control and regular eye examinations as advised by healthcare professionals.

RAG Safety Note:
This information is for general educational context in the MedVision prototype. It should not be used alone to diagnose or rule out eye disease.
