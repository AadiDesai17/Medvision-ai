Condition: Severe Diabetic Retinopathy

Dataset: EyePACS
Category: Diabetic retinopathy grade

General Information:
Severe diabetic retinopathy is an advanced stage of non-proliferative diabetic retinopathy. At this stage, extensive retinal blood vessel abnormalities can occur and the risk of progression to proliferative disease is increased.

Common Features:
Retinal photographs may show extensive hemorrhages, microaneurysms, and other vascular abnormalities. The exact appearance varies between patients.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
The condition is associated with diabetes. Longer duration of diabetes and factors such as poor blood glucose control and high blood pressure can increase the risk of progression.

General Management:
Patients generally require closer ophthalmic monitoring and appropriate management of diabetes and other risk factors. Treatment decisions depend on retinal findings and clinical assessment.
