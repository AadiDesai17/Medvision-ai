Condition: Glioma

Dataset: Brain Tumor MRI
Category: Brain tumor

General Information:
Glioma is a type of tumor that develops from glial cells or related cells in the brain or spinal cord. Gliomas vary widely in their biological behavior and grade.

Common Features:
Symptoms depend on the tumor's location and size. Possible symptoms include headaches, seizures, changes in vision, weakness, difficulty with speech, or changes in memory and behavior.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
The exact cause of most gliomas is not known. Certain inherited conditions and previous exposure to ionizing radiation can increase risk in some people.

General Management:
Evaluation may involve neurological examination, MRI, and sometimes tissue sampling. Treatment depends on tumor type and grade and may include surgery, radiation therapy, chemotherapy, targeted treatment, or observation.
