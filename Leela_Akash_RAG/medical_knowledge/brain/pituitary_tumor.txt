Condition: Pituitary Tumor

Dataset: Brain Tumor MRI
Category: Brain tumor

General Information:
A pituitary tumor is an abnormal growth arising in or around the pituitary gland. Many pituitary tumors are benign and may affect hormone production or nearby structures.

Common Features:
Symptoms depend on hormone production and tumor size. Possible symptoms include headaches, vision problems, fatigue, and hormonal changes.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
The exact cause of most pituitary tumors is not known. Some rare inherited conditions can increase the risk of developing pituitary tumors.

General Management:
Management depends on the tumor type, hormone activity, size, symptoms, and growth. Options may include observation, medicines, surgery, or radiation therapy.
