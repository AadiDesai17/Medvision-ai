Condition: Meningioma

Dataset: Brain Tumor MRI
Category: Brain tumor

General Information:
A meningioma is a tumor that develops from the meninges, the protective membranes surrounding the brain and spinal cord. Many meningiomas grow slowly and are benign, although some can behave more aggressively.

Common Features:
Symptoms depend on location and size. Possible symptoms include headaches, seizures, vision changes, weakness, or problems with balance and coordination.

Common Causes and Risk Factors:
The exact cause is often unknown. Risk factors can include certain genetic conditions and previous exposure to radiation. Meningiomas are more common in adults.

General Management:
Management depends on tumor size, location, growth, symptoms, and biological characteristics. Options may include observation with imaging, surgery, or radiation therapy.
